Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

QuoteReels is an automated content publishing service. It publishes originally-created videos to the TikTok account of its owner and does not operate a public-facing service that collects viewer data.

What we collect

The service stores only its own authentication tokens for the connected account. It does not collect, store, process, or share any personal information from TikTok users or viewers.

TikTok API access

The service uses the TikTok Content Posting API exclusively to upload videos to the owner own account, with permissions limited to uploading videos and reading basic profile information.
